Chess and Go on impossible boards.

Glue the edges of a board together and the board becomes a surface: a torus, a Klein bottle, a projective plane. The rules never change. Pick a surface, watch it fold, then play on it. Read about the math.

01

Glue the edges

Each board says what happens when a piece walks off an edge: it wraps to the far side, comes back mirrored, turns a corner, or stops at a wall.

02

Get a surface

Matching colours on the edges are glued together. Fold the board until they meet and you get the surface the game is really played on.

03

Same rules, new game

Starting positions and rules are identical everywhere. Some surfaces produce sharp games; on the torus, chess is checkmate before the first move.
