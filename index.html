1 Board

Fundamental domain

One copy of the board. Each edge is identified with another edge (by translation, glide reflection, or quarter turn), reflects onto itself, or is a wall. A piece crossing an identified edge re-enters at its image.

2 Tiling

Tiling of the plane

The plane tiled by images of the board under its symmetry group; each copy is translated, rotated, or reflected per its edge identification. A line of movement is a straight line in the plane, projected back onto the board.

3 Surface

Quotient surface

The board with identified edges joined: a torus, a Klein bottle, a projective plane. No per-surface rule changes or setup shifts; degenerate outcomes are reported as they fall. Torus chess is checkmate at move zero.
